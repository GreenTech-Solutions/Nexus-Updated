**A fork of [Nexus Extended Promethium Endgame](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Nexus) by Karu_Kiruna, updated for Factorio 2.1.** All credit for the mod goes to Karu_Kiruna. It is a stopgap until the original has a 2.1 release; then this fork is deprecated. Source: [GreenTech-Solutions/Nexus-Updated](https://github.com/GreenTech-Solutions/Nexus-Updated).

Expands the promethium endgame and adds another planet, Nexus, with new production steps and resources.

The fork is three mods, as the original: Nexus-Updated, [Nexus-Threat-Updated](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Nexus-Threat-Updated) (storms, instability and the shield) and [Nexus-Graphics-Updated](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Nexus-Graphics-Updated). It changes only what 2.1 needs, bug fixes and what a save needs to switch over; the balance is the original's. The list is in the changelog.

## Switching a save from Nexus

1. Install Nexus-Updated (it brings the other two) **before** you load the save. A save loaded without them loses everything of Nexus: buildings, items and the planet itself.
2. Load the save. Prototype and setting names are unchanged, so buildings, items, research and map settings stay. The shield stabilizers keep their tier; the instability and the shield start from 0.
